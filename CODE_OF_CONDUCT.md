# Code of conduct

Treat people respectfully regardless of background, identity, or experience. Discuss the work without attacking the person. Welcome questions and corrections, and avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and unwanted contact. Never publish another person's private information without permission.

These standards apply in project repositories, discussions, reviews, and interactions where someone represents the project. `taibenvenuti` is the sole maintainer and may remove abusive content, warn participants, or restrict participation. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is not acceptable.

For violations of GitHub's policies, including conduct by the maintainer, [report abuse privately to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Choose the option that reports to GitHub, not to repository maintainers. GitHub applies its own policies; it is not an independent officer for this project's code of conduct.

The project has no independent conduct officer or dedicated private inbox for project-only concerns. Do not post sensitive reports or identifying details in issues or pull requests. Revisit private reporting and independent handling as the project grows.
