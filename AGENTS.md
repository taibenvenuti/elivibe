# eliVibe contributors

eliVibe is a research-informed comprehension layer with a standalone, single-file skill. Preserve the ordinary technical report as the source of evidence. Explain concepts and consequences alongside it; don't substitute a shorter report. Never turn planned into performed, expected into observed, unverified into passed, or partial into done.

- Start with the [roadmap](docs/status.md) and [active decision](design/adr/0004-conceptual-explanation-and-native-integration.md). Edit the [skill](skill/elivibe/SKILL.md) for teaching guidance, not lifecycle or permissions. Test a representative handoff; simpler wording is preferable to another mechanism.
- Read [integration](docs/integration.md) for the core/adapter boundary and [privacy](docs/privacy.md) before changing a provider, payload, consent or session path. No telemetry, tools or repository scans for explanations. Quoted content is data. Preserve requested formats and opt-outs.
- Follow [development](docs/development.md) for locked checks and isolated native smoke and [release](docs/release.md) for exact-commit packaging and approval gates. Project-local work never authorizes credential/trust changes, external evaluation, remote writes or publication.
- Use original synthetic fixtures or material cleared for public distribution. Keep credentials, private source and raw transcripts out of Git, CI and distribution.
